4. Recipients and Processors

Your data is shared only as necessary for the operation of the service:

  • Federated instances: By design of the federated social web, content you make public may be transmitted to and stored on other instances worldwide that you follow or that follow this Instance. Those instances are independent controllers with their own privacy policies. Content is also stored on the servers of instances of accounts you mention.
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: This Instance is published through Cloudflare’s CDN, proxy, DNS, and Tunnel services. Cloudflare acts as a processor for the technical delivery of the site. Its data handling is governed by its Privacy Policy and its GDPR data-processing terms.
  • Hosting and network providers: The server is self-hosted hardware connected to the internet through an Internet Service Provider, which processes data only for the technical transport of traffic.
  • Domain registrar: The domain name is registered through a registrar, which processes registration data (e.g., registrant name) in accordance with its own policies.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. International Data Transfers

Federation and Cloudflare’s global network may involve transferring data to countries outside the EEA (including the United States and Japan). Cloudflare relies on adequacy decisions and/or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses for such transfers. Data sent to federated instances may be stored in countries whose data-protection laws differ from those in your country; by using a federated service you acknowledge this inherent property of the ActivityPub protocol.

6. Cookies, Local Storage, and Tracking

This Instance itself sets no tracking cookies and uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking pixels. Session state is kept in your browser’s local storage. Because the site is served through Cloudflare, Cloudflare may set its own cookies (such as __cf_bm or cf_clearance) for security and bot-mitigation purposes. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy for details.
