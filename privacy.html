Privacy Policy

sharkey.inari-lost-shrine.net (hereinafter "this Instance") is operated personally by the administrator YomiPlush. This policy sets out how personal information is handled on this Instance, in accordance with Japanese law (including the Act on the Protection of Personal Information).

1. Information Collected

This Instance automatically collects and stores the following information to provide the service:

InformationPurpose
Account information (username, email address, password hash, etc.)Account management
Content of posts and uploaded filesProvision of the service
Access logs (IP address, User-Agent, access time, etc.)Security and prevention of misuse
Cookies and session informationMaintaining login state

2. Purposes of Use

  • To provide and operate this service
  • To communicate with other instances via federation (ActivityPub)
  • To prevent unauthorized access, spam, and other misuse
  • To improve the service

3. Disclosure to Third Parties

Collected information will not be disclosed to third parties except in the following cases:

  • When posts and other content are delivered to federated instances via the federation protocol
  • When required by law
  • When the individual has given consent

This Instance is published via Cloudflare services (Cloudflare Tunnel), and part of the traffic passes through Cloudflare. For Cloudflare's data handling, please refer to the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention Period

Collected information is retained only for the period necessary to provide the service. Access logs are stored for a certain period for security purposes and then deleted.

5. Security

Appropriate security measures are taken to prevent unauthorized access, leakage, loss, or damage to collected information.

6. Analytics and External Services

This Instance does not use external analytics services that track user behavior.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the Administrator via the federated account (@YomiPlush@sharkey.inari-lost-shrine.net).
